" Get To Know Me "

I’m Fadi Kazaan a 29-year-old professional Barber and Hairstylist from Syria, My journey began in Syria in 2014, where my passion first took shape. It later continued in Lebanon and is now evolving in Dubai.

Holder of the Guinness World Records title for “The Most Consecutive Beards Shaved/Trimmed in One Hour (Team)” with 191 completed trims, delivering a private barber experience defined by precision, consistency, and attention to detail.

Confidence and self-expression have always been a major part of who I am. Over the years, I discovered that personal style and appearance play a powerful role in how we present ourselves, communicate our ideas, and leave lasting impressions.
That realization became the foundation of my passion and career. Today, my mission is to help people look and feel their absolute best. As a professional barber and hairstylist, I focus on creating unique, personalized looks that reflect each client’s identity, confidence, and individuality.
Beyond styling, I see my role as an advisor — someone who helps clients enhance their image and stand out with authenticity and confidence.
